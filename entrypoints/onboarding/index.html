Hey, I'm Nomi.

I live beside your browser. I never click, never type, never take control — I just share the workspace, notice how your day goes, and remember it so you don't have to.

What I notice

Which sites we spend time on and for how long. On LeetCode, I also cheer when a submission is accepted. That's it.

What I never touch

Keystrokes, passwords, messages, page contents. Banking, health, and mail sites are invisible to me — and you can add any site to that list.

Where it all lives

On this device, encrypted. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry. Export it or erase it any time — it's yours.

You'll find me in the corner of every page — drag me anywhere, tap me to talk. If you ever want quiet, the popup has a pause button.
