# Nomi

**Your digital companion that quietly understands your day.**

Nomi is an AI companion that lives beside your browser. It never clicks, never types, never takes control. It quietly shares your workspace, learns your habits, remembers your day, and speaks up only when it matters — then celebrates with you when you finally crack that problem.

Not another AI assistant. Just a companion.

- 🪟 A floating glass avatar that breathes, blinks, and glances at your cursor
- 🧠 Daily memory: "Today felt productive. You solved five LeetCode questions…"
- 🔍 Ask it things: "When did I last study graphs?"
- 🔒 Local-first: encrypted on your device, no cloud recording, no telemetry
