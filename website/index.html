The idea

Everything you do online, held in one quiet mind.

Notices

Quiet attention

It sees the shape of your day: the deep stretches, the tab spirals, the late nights. It never reads a keystroke.

Understands

Context, not clicks

Not “you opened GitHub,” but “42 minutes reviewing a pull request, after two problems.” It knows the work, not just the site.

Remembers

So you don't have to

Ask it anything about your days and it answers from what it actually saw: grounded, specific, never made up.

Intelligence

It doesn't log. It reasons.

Before it answers, Nomi thinks in steps. It consults your memory, your projects, and the page in front of you, then says one useful thing.

Reasoning engine

Thinks before it speaks

A step-by-step mind that pulls from what it knows: your history, your open loops, your documents. Every reply is grounded in the truth of your day.

Intent

Holds your loose ends

“I'll read this later.” “I should reply.” Kept as unfinished intentions, not lost in forty tabs.

Learning

Learns you slowly

Your peak hours, your stack, your usual distractions. Learned from evidence over days, never a single event.

Projects

Groups your work

Résumé, portfolio, a hackathon: every page quietly sorted into what it's really for.

Care

Notices when you push too hard

A late-night marathon with no breaks earns a gentle check-in with the evidence, never a guilt trip.

Everything it does

A quiet toolkit.

Voice

Talk to it. It talks back

Hold and speak, even in Hinglish. It answers out loud in a natural voice.

The bag

Save & recall anything

“Keep this.” Later: “what did I save about rate limiting?” Found, and opened with a tap.

Reminders

By time, or by place

“In twenty minutes,” or “when I open Gmail.” It understands “in an hour,” and fixes itself when you correct it.

On the page

Summarize · Explain · Read aloud · Translate

“Summarize this.” “Explain this error.” “Read it to me.” “Translate to Hindi.” Grounded in what's on your screen, and it never reads a URL out loud.

Focus

Focus with me

“Twenty-five minutes.” It holds the clock and stays perfectly quiet until the bell.

Instant

Math · Time · Dates

“15% of 2400,” “3pm PST in IST,” “days until August 15.” Offline, instant.

Never in the way

Resize · Move · Hide

Drag it anywhere, size it small to large, or let it tuck into the corner and doze until you need it.

Privacy

It never leaves your device.

No cloud. No accounts. No telemetry. Encrypted on your machine, and yours to erase, key included.

On-device AES-256 No accounts No telemetry Banking · health · mail stay invisible
How to start

Running in a minute.

Load it

Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and load the Nomi build.

STEP 02

Add a brain

Paste a free Gemini key in Settings for the smartest, reasoning replies.

Nomi appears on every page. Tap to talk, or just let it remember.
